About

A modern nomad with a camera

For more than five years I've lived on the road, out of a Subaru Forester and now a Hyundai Ioniq 5, following the light across the United States.

Before that, and in between, I've traveled to nearly 30 countries. Photography and film are how I keep the places I pass through.

I shoot on the ground with a Sony A7C and in the air with a DJI Mavic 3 and Avata. If you need a place captured, there's a good chance I'm already headed somewhere nearby.
